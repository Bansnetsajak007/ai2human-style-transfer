As the use of AI rapidly expands across industries, it is also introducing a new wave of security concerns that cannot be ignored. Additionally, malicious actors can exploit AI itself—using techniques like deepfakes, automated phishing, or adversarial attacks—to manipulate systems and deceive users at scale. There is also the growing problem of model vulnerabilities, where attackers can reverse-engineer or poison models to produce harmful or biased outputs. As AI becomes more integrated into critical infrastructure such as healthcare, finance, and national security, even small weaknesses can lead to significant real-world consequences. This makes it essential to prioritize robust security measures, transparent model design, and strict governance to ensure that AI remains both powerful and safe.

The evolution from classical and theoretical deep learning to modern agentic AI reflects a shift from understanding intelligence as a mathematical function approximator to viewing it as an active, goal-driven system. Classical deep learning focuses on well-defined architectures—like neural networks trained via backpropagation—grounded in theory, optimization, and statistical learning, where models passively learn patterns from data and produce outputs. In contrast, modern agentic AI builds on these foundations but moves toward systems that can plan, reason, use tools, and take actions in dynamic environments, often iterating over multiple steps to achieve a goal. Instead of just predicting the next token or label, agentic systems orchestrate decision-making processes, interact with external systems, and adapt in real time. While theoretical deep learning emphasizes rigor, guarantees, and understanding model behavior, agentic AI prioritizes capability, autonomy, and practical problem-solving—sometimes at the cost of interpretability. This shift highlights a broader transition in AI: from static intelligence defined by equations to more fluid, interactive systems that behave increasingly like agents operating in the real world.
